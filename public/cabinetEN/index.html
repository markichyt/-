What this is. A static demo: 25 cabinet pages saved from a live logged-in session together with the styles, fonts, scripts and images. No internet needed.
Buying a lead works end to end. On «Buy a lead» press «Respond»: the lead is reserved for you for 90 seconds with a countdown, showing the price with the subscription discount and your balance after payment. Confirming charges the money, the lead moves to «My leads» together with the client's contacts, and the counter and balance in the cabinet update. If the reservation runs out it is released, with the option to reserve again; if there is not enough money the demo offers to top up the balance. The state is kept in the browser, and «Reset demo state» puts everything back to the start.
What else works. All navigation through the menu and tabs, filters, search, expanding cards, accordions, modal windows, calendars, text editors — everything the live cabinet does on the browser side.
What is missing. The rest of the actions that go to the server on the live site: saving forms, payment, withdrawals, logging out. On such a click the demo shows a hint instead of sending a request.
