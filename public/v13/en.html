Frequently asked questions

What happens after I sign up?
Getting started takes just a few minutes. Complete your profile or upload your resume, choose a subscription plan, and connect your social media accounts. Our AI begins building your profile, creating content, and preparing your marketing tools so you can start attracting new clients.
Do I have to record videos myself?
No. Upload one professional photo and a short voice sample. Your AI avatar creates videos and social media content for you, so you never have to film or edit anything yourself.
Where do the client leads come from?
Client opportunities come from multiple sources, including your published content, search engines, social media, advertising campaigns, and the CONSULTANT marketplace. Every lead generated through your profile is delivered directly to your dashboard.
How does lead purchasing work?
Each available lead includes the practice area, location, and price before you purchase it. Once you buy a lead, the contact information becomes available immediately. Every purchased lead is exclusive and is not sold to multiple attorneys.
I don't know anything about marketing. Is this platform still for me?
Absolutely. CONSULTANT is designed for attorneys, not marketers. The platform helps create content, publish posts, manage advertising, and support your online visibility automatically. Your primary focus remains practicing law and serving your clients.
How soon can I expect results?
Your profile and content can be published within the first few days after setup. If you purchase leads, you can begin contacting potential clients immediately. Organic growth from content and search visibility builds over time as your online presence grows.
Is client communication secure?
Yes. Client conversations take place inside the platform's secure messaging system, helping you keep communications organized in one professional workspace.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. Annual plans include a 10% discount.
What if there are few requests in my city or practice area?
Clients come through several channels at once: paid requests from the dashboard, free inbound from answering questions, and organic Google traffic. In the quiz we'll estimate the potential of your specific practice by area and location.
Can I get clients for free?
Yes. Answer clients' questions — the one your answer convinces can pick you to handle their case, with nothing deducted. Plus your resume-based SEO page brings free traffic from Google.
Do I control what's published in my name?
You set the direction, tone, and topics — content is created for your specialty. Client communication runs through the platform's secure messenger with call recording.
Does this comply with attorney advertising rules?
You publish educational content and your real profile and experience — with no promises of outcomes. You control the format of your promotion.
